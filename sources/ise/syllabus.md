# 20MCN401 — Industrial Safety Engineering · Syllabus (verbatim)

**Saintgits College of Engineering (Autonomous) · B.Tech CSE · Semester 7 · 2020 scheme**
Course credit **0** (mandatory non-credit course) · L-T-P 2-1-0 · **ESE weight 20% per module**

## Course objectives

1. Familiarise with the theories of accident causation and preventive measures of industrial accidents.
2. Give an idea of the importance of personal protective equipment and housekeeping.
3. Familiarise with the different issues in construction industries.
4. Impart knowledge of hazards associated with machines and mechanical material handling.
5. Give an idea of chemical hazards and hazard identification tools in different industries.

## Course outcomes

| CO | Statement |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Describe the theories of accident causation and preventive measures of industrial accidents |
| CO2 | Explain PPE, its selection, safety performance indicators, and the importance of housekeeping |
| CO3 | Explain different issues in construction industries |
| CO4 | Describe hazards associated with machines and mechanical material handling |
| CO5 | Utilise hazard identification tools in different industries |

## One-line course content

Need for safety · safety and productivity · theories of accident causation · safety organisation · types of PPEs · standards for PPEs · monitoring safety performance · housekeeping · work permit system · construction industry safety · ergonomics hazards · machinery safeguard · material handling equipment safety · hazard identification · types of hazards · classification of fire · inventory analysis · preliminary hazard analysis · HAZOP · control of chemical hazards · MSDS
