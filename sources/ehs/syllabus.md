# 20CET453 — Environmental Health and Safety · Syllabus (verbatim)

**Saintgits College of Engineering (Autonomous) · B.Tech Civil · Semester 7 · 2020 scheme**
Credits 3 (L-T-P 3-0-0) · Course content 36 hrs · 2 internal tests of 2 hrs · **ESE weight 20% per module**

## Course objectives

1. Communicate about occupational health and safety
2. Impart knowledge of chemical hazards
3. Introduce industrial safety standards
4. Introduce water and air pollution
5. Impart knowledge about the safe working environment

## Course outcomes

| CO | Statement | Bloom |
|---|---|---|
| CO1 | Identify the toxicology and occupational health associated with industries | 2 |
| CO2 | Identify chemical and microbial agents that originate in the environment and can affect human health | 2 |
| CO3 | Describe measures to ensure safety in the construction industry | 2 |
| CO4 | Describe the effect of air and water pollution on the environment | 2 |
| CO5 | Describe safety measures against various environmental hazards | 2 |

## One-line course content

Occupational health and toxicology — occupational diseases — toxic materials and substances used in work — industrial hygiene — chemical hazards — biological hazards — infectious diseases — noise and its effects — safety in construction industry — electric hazards — radiation and its effects — air and water pollution — waste management — safe working environment — first-aid appliances — prevention of pollution.
