# START HERE — 20CET453 Environmental Health and Safety

**No ESE paper and no question bank exist for this course.** The only past questions are three internal papers (2 hrs / 50 marks), and they never once asked a Module V question — so they are evidence of *style*, not of scope. The syllabus is the scope.

Each module is 20% of the ESE. Module 5 has never been examined internally — that is exactly why it is the cheapest 20% in the paper: nobody revises it.

## Answer shapes this subject is built around

| Marks | What earns them |
|---|---|
| **3** | three numbered points: define, classify, one example. No introduction. |
| **5** | five points, or four plus a small labelled sketch — internal papers use this for "explain the hazards…" |
| **7–8** | `Structure` → classified bullets (cause → effect → control) → one diagram. This is the internal Part B shape. |
| **14** | the essay: definition, classification table, effects with a diagram, control measures, one Indian standard/example. |

No numerical problems appear in any paper. Every question is descriptive: define, classify, list effects, list controls.

## What the internal papers actually asked (and what it tells you)

Repeated across papers: **preventive measures of occupational diseases**, **noise and its effects**, **electrical hazards on site**, **scaffolding precautions**, **MSDS**, **clean coal**, **silicosis/asbestosis**, **air pollution effects on plants**.

Never asked: anything from Module V, water-pollution control methods, individual's role in conservation, safety inspection. That gap is a syllabus gap, not a signal.

## How to work through a module

1. **§1 coverage map** — check the syllabus bullet is actually answered; nothing is out of scope.
2. **§2 concepts** — one block per bullet: definition, classification, effect, control.
3. **§3 answer bank** — practise the 3- and 5-mark shapes out loud; write one 7/8-mark answer per module by hand.
4. **§4 definitions and numbers** — the only things that must be word-perfect.
5. **§5 diagrams** — a labelled sketch is usually worth 2 of the 8 marks.
