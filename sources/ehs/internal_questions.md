# Internal question papers — 20CET453 EHS (the only past questions available)

Three papers from the department. Two formats appear; both are 2 hours / 50 marks. **No module-5 question exists in any of them** — module 5 was never examined internally, so absence there is *not* evidence that it will be skipped in the ESE. Syllabus is the only reliable scope.

## Format observed

| Paper | Part A | Part B | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internal Test 1, Sep 2024 | 6 × 3 = 18 | 4 × 8 = 32 | 50 |
| Special Retest II, Oct 2025 | 4 × 5 = 20 | 7 + 8 + 7 + 8 = 30 | 50 |
| Retest I, Oct 2026 | 4 × 5 = 20 | 7 + 8 + 7 + 8 = 30 | 50 |

Verb style: *describe, explain, elaborate, discuss, outline, enlist, identify, categorize, formulate, investigate, summarize*. No numerical problems in any paper. Every question is answerable as (a) a definition, (b) a classified list, (c) effects, (d) control measures.

## Internal Test 1 — September 2024

**Part A (3 marks each)**
1. Describe silicosis.
2. Describe the relevance of material safety data sheet.
3. Enlist the objectives of occupational health.
4. List the types of biological agents with examples.
5. Outline the routes of entry of chemicals into the body.
6. Define (a) aerosols (b) fumes.

**Part B (8 marks each)**
7. Summarize preventive measures of various occupational diseases.
8. Discuss the health and safety issues in any two industries.
9. Elaborate on noise pollution and its effects.
10. Describe the measures to protect from hazardous chemicals.

**Bias of the question set:** modules I–IV only, and inside them the same 8–10 topics cycle. Module V (safety inspection, first aid, welfare facilities, PPE, individual's role in conservation and pollution control) never appeared — but it carries 20% of the ESE and three syllabus lines of its own. Treat it as first-class.

**ESE format is not in this folder.** The college's 100-mark 3-hour papers for other courses run Part A (10 × 3, two per module) + Part B (5 × 14, one per module). Plan for that shape, but every answer in these kits is also written so it can be cut down to a 3-mark or a 5/7/8-mark internal answer.
